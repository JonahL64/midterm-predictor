**Cook_PVI** = how much more a district leans politically compared to the country

**District PVI Average** = (0.75 * r_new) + (0.25 * r_old)

r_new = recent presidential election result leading party share
r_old = previous presidential election result leading party share

Results expressed as D+n or R+n (D+4, R+6, for example)

If a district falls within 0.5 points of the national average in either direction, it is designated as **EVEN**.
