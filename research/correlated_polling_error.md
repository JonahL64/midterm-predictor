**Donald Trump was behind ~7% in certain states that he ended up winning in 2016.**

#### National Polls Were Accurated
- National polls were generally accurate
- Polling issues at the state level:
  - Late move towards Trump by undecided voters in swing state
  - Inaccurate weighting
  - Unexpectedly high turnout in rural areas

#### How a Poll Starts
Polls start with a random sample from a population within a country. Cell phones have made building that random sample harder or more expensive. Pew Research's response rate fell from 35% to 6%. 

Pollsters also have to spend more money on labor for manually dialing phone numbers. You used to be able to use an auto-dialer. You can't use the Internet because you're excluding people without the Internet and people who might not be on your certain web page at a time.

#### Weighting
Pollsters use weighting to adjust the proportions of voting blocs to national proportions.

Many pollsters were slow to catch onto key shifts in the electorate, like educational attainment. 

America is far more polarized among gender lines and the lines of education. White men without a college degree and white women with a college degree exploded apart in terms of political stance.
